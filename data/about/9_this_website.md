# About This Website

This website serves as my personal website & portfolio.

It is a space where I showcase my projects, share work that I have built or contributed to, and document things I am learning along the way.

The site highlights selected projects, technical interests, and experiences. 
It is intended to show visitors an overview of my skills, the technologies I work with, and updates on current projects.

Beyond being a portfolio, this site is also a personal space on the internet. It functions as a playground for ideas, experiments (design & technical), and small features that I find interesting to build.

Many of these additions are not necessarily meant to be practical—they exist as a way for me to explore new techniques, test ideas, and spend time building things purely for creativity. As the site evolves, new features, experiments, and small surprises may appear throughout different pages.

In short, this website is functions as a portfolio and secondly as a creative sandbox where I can share my work while continuing to experiment and build.
