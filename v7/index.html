Backstory

Hello, I am Chester.

I am a self-taught developer who started programming in ~2018-2019.

My love and passion 💖 for programming and computers 💻 started after I discovered minecraft mods.
It simply amazed me that you could just do that to add in features to another software.
The installation process for the mods, from the perspective of me at that time, was also rather technical 🔧 and rather scary.

These experiences made me curious about how computers and software in general worked.
It lit a fire 🔥 in my heart, kickstarting my journey into the world of computers.

That is it. I can’t think of anymore words to fill up this section.
